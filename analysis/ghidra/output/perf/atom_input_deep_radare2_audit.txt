Contract correction
===================

Both device branches test Acquire's HRESULT (0 or 1 accepted), but neither
checks Poll's or GetDeviceState's HRESULT. Keyboard GetDeviceState is called
at 0x00A231E1, followed by an unconditional jump. Mouse GetDeviceState is called
at 0x00A23292, also followed by an unconditional jump. The state-clearing
branches are reached on the earlier acquisition failure, not on a failed
GetDeviceState. The prior ledger's "acquisition/state retrieval fails"
description was too broad. Keep that historical raw ledger unchanged and use
this correction with docs/fnv_actor_input_contract.md.

This proves missing native error handling, not an observed failure in the
owner's workload. Atom's outer sampler call chains a void thiscall function
and receives no device HRESULT. A future intervention must still establish
the live-provider and caller contract; these excerpts do not authorize one.
