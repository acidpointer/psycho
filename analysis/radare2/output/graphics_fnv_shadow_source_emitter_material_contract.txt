Questions
=========

1. Which exact native material fields can distinguish an emissive fixture
   shell from an ordinary point-shadow occluder?
2. Can OMV relate a geometry leaf to the selected native point light without

Direct binary evidence
======================

NiMaterialProperty constructor @ 0x00A75650
------------------------------------------------

The constructor installs vtable 0x0109D6C4 at 0x00A7565A. Its initialization
sequence establishes the fields used by OMV:

  0x00A7566E  fst dword [esi + 0x28]
  0x00A75671  fst dword [esi + 0x2C]
  0x00A75674  fst dword [esi + 0x30]
  0x00A7567A  mov dword [esi + 0x34], 0
  0x00A75696  fstp dword [esi + 0x38]
  0x00A7569B  fst dword [esi + 0x3C]
  0x00A7569E  fstp dword [esi + 0x40]

The FPU value at +0x28/+0x2C/+0x30 is zero. The pointer at +0x34 is null.
The value at +0x38 is loaded from 0x0101E570. The constructor writes one to
+0x3C and +0x40.

Header corroboration
====================

The modern NVR New Vegas header at
.research/TESReloaded10-master/src/NewVegas/nvse/GameNi.h:3028-3040 names the
same 0x4C-byte layout:

  +0x28  NiColor emit
  +0x34  NiColor* pExternalEmittance
  +0x38  float fShine
  +0x3C  float fAlpha
  +0x40  float fEmitMult

The independently recorded player-coverage contract proves that
NiAVObject +0x18 is the parent NiNode pointer:

  analysis/radare2/output/perf/graphics_fnv_motion_blur_player_coverage_contract.txt

That parent link is sufficient for a bounded, synchronous ancestry walk. It
does not establish ownership beyond the serialized common-shadow invocation
and must not be retained as a native pointer.

Evidence classification
=======================

Proven by the current executable:

- NiMaterialProperty owns inline RGB fields at +0x28/+0x2C/+0x30, a pointer
  field at +0x34, alpha at +0x3C, and a scalar initialized to one at +0x40.
- NiAVObject parent ownership is represented by the pointer at +0x18, as
  established in the cited radare2 artifact.

Corroborated by the modern NVR header:

- the inline triplet is material emission;
- +0x34 is the optional external-emittance color;
- +0x40 is the emission multiplier; and
- NiMaterialProperty has the exact 0x4C-byte layout expected by these offsets.

Reasoned rendering policy awaiting runtime acceptance:

- a geometry leaf may be excluded from one point cube only when it is beneath
  that light's immediate scene parent, has positive effective material
  emission, and its finite world bound encloses the light source;
- unrelated nearby geometry, non-emissive fixture bodies, lampshades, and
  walls remain casters; and
- a non-null external-emittance pointer supplies the effective color instead
  of the inline triplet.

Safe intervention boundary
==========================

The existing common-shadow hook serializes this work and consumes every
engine pointer before the native tail resumes. OMV may copy the selected
light's immediate parent as an integer in its transaction-local light record,
walk bounded parent links during geometry classification, and read the live
material fields there. It must not retain or mutate a material, geometry,
parent node, or external-emittance pointer.

Invalid, non-finite, overflowing, detached, non-emissive, or non-enclosing
inputs fail closed by retaining the caster. This narrow failure rule is
essential: a broad distance-based source cutoff would remove legitimate
occluders and recreate wall leaks.
